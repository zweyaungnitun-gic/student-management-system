Who Is It For? Who Should Join?

For all young people who want to go to Japan as IT professionals

  • 1) Recent graduates or those within 3 years of graduation
  • 2) Those interested in IT who have not yet graduated
  • 3) Those who want to start learning IT & Japanese from zero level
  • 4) Those with an IT background who want to switch to the IT field
  • 5) Students who have graduated from language schools in Japan
  • 6) Those who want to work as IT Engineers in Japan

What Will You Learn?

Until you reach the IT workplace, GIC Career Gateway Japan IT Education & Bootcamp

  • 1) Liberal Arts
  • 2) IT Full Cycle Development Bootcamp
  • 3) IT Workplace Japanese Language Training
  • 4) On Job Training

Why Choose GIC Career Gateway?

  • A Liberal Arts, IT & Japanese Training Center founded by an IT company based in Tokyo, Japan
  • After completing the course, we create opportunities to work within the GIC network
  • GIC staff can support those who want to work in the GIC network to carry out OJT projects
  • Unlike other schools, we teach from the basics to practical application
  • Gain experience working in an actual workplace
  • Not only for IT people, but also developing the skills, experience and intelligence that every IT professional should have
